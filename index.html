About Hackathon

Building India’s Next Generation Hardware

Hosted by the Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering at St. Peter's Engineering College, TEKZITHON 2027 is a prestigious 36-hour national hardware hackathon designed to test practical engineering capability.

From Schematic to Silicon

Unlike software hackathons where virtual code suffices, TEKZITHON 2027 demands physical builds. Teams design circuits, etch or wire PCBs, solder components, write firmware, and validate live hardware sensors.

Industry & Academic Mentorship

Teams receive real-time technical guidance from experienced hardware practitioners and faculty experts across analog design, RF systems, IoT firmware, and robotic kinematics.

Fair & Transparent Evaluation

Submitting your abstract is 100% free. Only shortlisted projects advance to the live build, where prototypes are judged on novelty, feasibility, engineering rigor, and real-world impact.

Prototyping Arsenal

Hardware Components in Action

Microcontrollers & SOCs

ARM Cortex, ESP32, STM32, Arduino boards, and RISC-V processors providing 32-bit real-time control, Wi-Fi/BLE telemetry, and dual-core computation.

ESP32 · STM32 · RP2040

Discrete & Power Electronics

Sensors & Biomedical Transducers

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about submissions, shortlisting, and hackathon participation.

Is abstract submission really 100% free?
Yes, absolutely! Submitting an abstract costs zero rupees. Any eligible student team can submit their project idea, keywords, and block diagram without any payment.
When do we pay the participation fee?
The participation fee of ₹399 per person is payable strictly only after your team has been officially shortlisted. If your abstract is not shortlisted, you do not pay anything.
How is the total team registration fee calculated?
The fee is dynamically calculated at ₹399 per confirmed participant. For a solo participant it is ₹399, for 2 members it is ₹798, for 3 members it is ₹1,197, and for a full team of 4 it is ₹1,596. There is no minimum or fixed ₹1,600 team fee.
What is the maximum team size?
A team can have a maximum of four (4) participants, including the team leader.
What are the key dates for TEKZITHON 2027?
- Abstract Submission Deadline: 15 December 2026
- Shortlist Announcement: 26 December 2026
- 36-Hour Hackathon Commences: 22 January 2027
What should the block diagram contain?
The block diagram should clearly illustrate the hardware architecture of your project: power supply, microcontrollers, input sensors, output actuators/displays, communication transceivers, and data flow.
